Payments

Give us an xpub or your Lightning node. We hand out a fresh address per invoice; funds settle to you. We take 1% from a prepaid balance.

Escrow

A 2-of-3 multisig holds the deal. Any two of payer, payee, and arbiter release it. The payer can always reclaim after a timeout. Holder keeps 2%.

Escrow without a middle-man meeting

  1. Create an escrow with the payer & payee keys. We derive a 2-of-3 deposit address.
  2. The payer funds it. Nobody can move the coins alone.
  3. Accept → payee + arbiter release to the payee. Deny → payer + arbiter refund. Either way the holder keeps 2%.
  4. Timeout → after the deadline the payer reclaims alone (1% fee).

Non-custodial, by construction

  • Payments settle to an address derived from your key. We hold no spend key.
  • Escrow funds sit in multisig; we hold at most the arbiter key — co-sign only, never move funds alone.
  • No IP logs, no customer data model, fresh address per invoice, a v3 onion service.

We do not mix or obscure the movement of funds — payments settle directly to merchant wallets.
