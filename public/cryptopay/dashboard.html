Escrow

How escrow works here (plain words)

The buyer's money goes into a Bitcoin lock that needs 2 of 3 keys to open: the buyer's, the seller's, and ours (the arbiter). Nobody holds the money — not us, not the seller. It sits in the lock until two keys agree.

  • Happy path — release: seller delivered, so the seller signs and we co-sign. Money → seller. Fee 2%.
  • Refund: buyer signs, we co-sign. Money → buyer. Fee 2%.
  • Safety net — timeout: after the deadline the buyer can take it back alone, even if we vanish. Fee 1%.
  • Dispute: either side flags it; we look at the case and co-sign whichever side is right. Until then, no one can touch the funds.

Our key can only ever add a second signature — it can never move funds by itself. You sign in your own wallet and send us only the signature, never a key. Why "non-custodial" matters →
